Nestsafe support

Help with sharing family photos safely.

Need a hand? Email hello@expresssite.com.au and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days.

How do I share a photo safely?

Open Photos, choose a photo (or up to 20), tap Share, then choose Nestsafe. You'll see what each photo gives away. Tap Fix all, then Share safely to send the clean copy.

I can't see Nestsafe in the Share menu

Tap Share, scroll the row of apps to the end and tap More. Tap Edit, then add Nestsafe to your favourites. In Nestsafe, Home › Show me how walks you through it.

What does Nestsafe look for?

Hidden location, date and camera details; house and letterbox numbers; street signs and addresses; Australian number plates; school names; and school logos and crests on uniforms. You can also turn on faces and other text in Settings. If Nestsafe misses something, tap "Blur something else" and draw over it.

Does Nestsafe change my original photos?

No. Nestsafe always makes a new, safe copy. Your originals stay exactly as they were.

How much does it cost?

Nestsafe needs a plan. Start with a 3-day free trial of the yearly plan: we'll remind you the day before it ends, and you won't be charged if you cancel before then. You can also choose a monthly plan, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your currency.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the end of the period. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open Nestsafe › Settings › Restore purchases.

Do my photos leave my phone?

Never. Everything happens on your iPhone. Read our privacy policy.
