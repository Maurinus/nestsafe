Privacy policy

Nestsafe · Effective 29 October 2026

The short version: Nestsafe doesn't collect, store or send any of your data. Your photos are checked and cleaned on your iPhone and never leave it. There's no account, no servers, no analytics and no advertising.

Your photos

When you share a photo to Nestsafe, or ask it to check or clean your library, the photo is read on your iPhone to find its hidden details (such as location) and visible clues (such as house numbers, street signs and number plates). Safe copies are made on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, and Nestsafe has no servers to upload to.

Nestsafe asks for access to your photo library only to check your recent photos and to save clean copies to a "Nestsafe" album. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos. Your original photos are never changed.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your settings, such as which clues to look for and the first name you gave (used only for greetings).
  • Whether you have an active plan (read from the App Store), so the Share menu knows too.
  • A small technical log of memory use (numbers only, no photos or personal details), kept to stop the share screen running out of memory. It never leaves your iPhone.

All of this is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to these purchases.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and ask to be reminded before it ends, Nestsafe schedules a reminder on your iPhone. It isn't sent from a server.

Children

Nestsafe is made for parents and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

Nestsafe includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and shares no data with anyone, because it has none to share.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. We'll never start collecting your photos.
